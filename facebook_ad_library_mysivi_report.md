## Executive Summary & Methodology

This report analyzes active acquisition creatives for **MySivi** (`mysivi.ai`), an AI-powered conversational English and job interview coaching platform operating in India.

The target Ad Library query is filtered by `sort_data[mode]=total_impressions&sort_data[direction]=desc`. In Meta performance marketing, impression-sorted active ads provide clear empirical signals of capital deployment: advertisers eliminate unprofitable ad sets within 48 to 72 hours. Creatives that have run continuously for weeks at the highest impression tier reflect proven return on ad spend (ROAS) and stable Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

To evaluate creatives objectively, each ad is analyzed across five weighted dimensions in the **AIDA-L Index**:
* **Hook Stopping Power (30%):** 0–3s visual pattern interrupt, thumb-stop rate, problem identification.
* **Body Copy Persuasion (25%):** Mechanism of action (Arya AI), objection elimination, social proof.
* **Call-to-Action Alignment (20%):** Clarity of directive, low friction barrier, intent match.
* **Creative Format Fit (10%):** Platform-native 9:16 vertical video, pacing, audio clarity.
* **Meta Scale Longevity (15%):** Active duration on Meta confirming real-world conversion stability.

## Strategic Growth Recommendations for MySivi

1. **Initiative 01 — Campus Recruitment Hook Iteration:**  
   Ad 01 is the scaling control. Produce 3 creative iterations testing placement-specific hooks: *"TCS aur Infosys ke interview round mein sabse zyada rejection kahan hote hain? Spoken English hesitation."*
2. **Initiative 02 — Vernacular Market Localization:**  
   Re-film the winning interview skit using native creators in **Tamil** and **Telugu**. South India accounts for over 60% of spoken English app demand, where Facebook CPMs are 25–35% lower.
3. **Initiative 03 — Repurpose Static Price Anchors:**  
   Retire Ad 05 as a standalone static banner. Instead, take its strong economic comparison (₹10/day vs. ₹15,000 coaching institutes) and attach it as a 5-second end-card bumper on video ads.
